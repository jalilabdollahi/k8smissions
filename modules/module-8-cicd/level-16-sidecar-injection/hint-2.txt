The step waits a fixed 5 seconds, which only works if the sidecar is faster than that. Nothing in Tekton makes a step wait for a sidecar, and sidecar readiness is not something to rely on here. The step must retry until the database answers, but not forever: bound the loop (a counter or `date` deadline in the script), and remember `kubectl explain task.spec.steps.timeout` and `taskrun.spec.timeout` as extra safety nets.
