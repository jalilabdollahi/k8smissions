The mapping lives in ConfigMap `hook-binding` as JSONPath-like paths into the payload; `body` is the whole JSON document. A GitHub push payload has the commit under `head_commit`, but check the exact key names in the payload shape (`head_commit` holds `id`, `message`, `timestamp`, ...). Also ask when the receiver reads that file: `kubectl explain pod.spec.volumes.configMap` tells you how ConfigMap volumes are refreshed, and the script's first lines show when it loads the file.
