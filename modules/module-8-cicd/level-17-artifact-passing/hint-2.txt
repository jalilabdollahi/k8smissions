Two separate layers. First, a Pipeline can only reference a result that the producer Task declares, under exactly that name: `$(tasks.<pipeline task>.results.<name>)`. Second, a declared result is only filled when something writes to its file, `$(results.<name>.path)`; a file such as `/workspace/digest.txt` lives and dies with the pod. After fixing the reference, start a new run and read what `deploy` complains about, with `kubectl logs` on its pod.
