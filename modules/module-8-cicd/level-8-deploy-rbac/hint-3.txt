Create a Role and a RoleBinding in namespace `shop-staging` (`kubectl -n shop-staging create role ...` and `... create rolebinding --serviceaccount=k8smissions:deploy-sa`) with verbs `get,list,watch,create,update,patch` on `deployments.apps` and on `services`. Check with `kubectl auth can-i` before starting a new run, since the first fix will reveal the second 403.
