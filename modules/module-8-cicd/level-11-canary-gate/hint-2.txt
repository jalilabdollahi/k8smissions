A Service sends traffic only to pods whose labels match its `spec.selector`, and the canary Deployment has its own selector and pod labels, defined inside the Pipeline's `deploy-canary` Task. Separately, nothing limits how long `canary-gate` may run: `kubectl explain pipeline.spec.tasks.timeout` (a PipelineTask `timeout`) is how a stuck gate turns into a failure, and a failed gate makes the pipeline skip `promote`.
