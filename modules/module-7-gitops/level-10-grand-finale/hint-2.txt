Three layers, in this order: where Argo reads from (`spec.source.targetRevision`), which files it feeds to Helm (`spec.source.helm.valueFiles`, `kubectl explain application.spec.source.helm`), and what the chart renders (`helm template grand-finale-app chart -f chart/<values file>`). After the Application turns green, stop trusting it: check the pods (`kubectl describe`, events about quota) and test the Service from the `probe` pod. Healthy only means Kubernetes thinks the pods are ready.
