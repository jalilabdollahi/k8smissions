A CRD with several versions needs `spec.conversion`. With `strategy: None` the API server only rewrites `apiVersion`, so fields that were renamed are lost. `strategy: Webhook` sends the objects to a service over HTTPS, and that needs a service reference (with its `path`), `conversionReviewVersions` and a `caBundle` that signed the converter's certificate. See `kubectl explain crd.spec.conversion.webhook`. The converter's pods and the CA it was issued under are in the namespace: check `kubectl -n k8smissions get deploy,svc,secret` and the converter's logs.
