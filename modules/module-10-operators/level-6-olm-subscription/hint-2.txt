Operator releases form an upgrade graph: a release declares which earlier one it `replaces`, and a release may only be installed on top of the one it replaces. A jump over a release is an upgrade nobody tested: if you try it, read the new pod's log (`kubectl logs`) for the reason it stops. The operator's code comes from the bundle ConfigMap mounted at `/bundle`, so "installing" a release means pointing the Deployment's volume at another bundle. Check the Widgets between steps with `kubectl get widgets -o yaml`.
