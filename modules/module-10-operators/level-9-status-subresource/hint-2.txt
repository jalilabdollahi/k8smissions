`/status` is a separate endpoint that a CRD only serves when `subresources.status` is enabled. Once it is, the rules change for everyone: updates to the main resource ignore `status`, and updates to `/status` ignore `spec`. Think about every program that writes status to a Backup, not only the one that logs errors, and read what `sla-reporter` logs and does (`kubectl -n k8smissions get cm sla-reporter-code -o yaml`). `kubectl patch --subresource=status` is the client-side equivalent.
