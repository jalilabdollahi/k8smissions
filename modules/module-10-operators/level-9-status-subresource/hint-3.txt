1) enable the subresource on the CRD version:
kubectl patch crd backups.backup.acme.io --type=json -p '[{"op":"add","path":"/spec/versions/0/subresources","value":{<SUBRESOURCE>: {}}}]'

2) then change what the reporter writes to, in the ConfigMap that holds its script (new Jobs pick the change up):
kubectl -n k8smissions edit configmap sla-reporter-code

Test with a one-off run: kubectl -n k8smissions create job sla-test --from=cronjob/sla-reporter
