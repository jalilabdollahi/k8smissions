Point the webhook at the right CA and the pod at the right certificate:

kubectl patch validatingwebhookconfiguration policy-guard --type=json -p '[{"op":"replace","path":"/webhooks/0/clientConfig/caBundle","value":"<BASE64 CA>"}]'
kubectl -n k8smissions patch deployment policy-guard --type=json -p '[{"op":"replace","path":"/spec/template/spec/volumes/<N>/secret/secretName","value":"<SECRET>"}]'

The Secret stores the CA already base64-encoded under `ca.crt`.
