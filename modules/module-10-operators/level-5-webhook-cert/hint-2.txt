There are two sides to a TLS connection. The API server (the client) trusts whatever CA is in the webhook's `clientConfig.caBundle`: `kubectl get validatingwebhookconfiguration policy-guard -o yaml`. The server pod presents the certificate from its Secret: look at the Deployment's volumes and inspect the PEM with `openssl x509 -noout -dates -issuer -subject` (decode it with `base64 -d`). The platform team's Secret `policy-guard-tls-reissued` has the new material. The server reads its certificate once, when it starts.
