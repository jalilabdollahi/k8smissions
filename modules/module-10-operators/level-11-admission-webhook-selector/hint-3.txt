Add an exclusion for the server's namespace to the selector, then nudge the stuck Deployment:

kubectl patch mutatingwebhookconfiguration pod-labeler --type=json -p '[{"op":"add","path":"/webhooks/0/namespaceSelector/matchExpressions","value":[{"key":"kubernetes.io/metadata.name","operator":"NotIn","values":["<NAMESPACE>"]}]}]'
kubectl -n labeler-system rollout restart deploy/pod-labeler

The ReplicaSet backs off between failed attempts, so the restart saves you the wait.
