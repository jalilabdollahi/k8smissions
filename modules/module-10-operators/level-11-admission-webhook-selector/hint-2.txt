The webhook has to answer for every pod it matches, including the pod of its own server, which cannot answer before it exists. With `failurePolicy: Fail` that deadlock never resolves. A `namespaceSelector` can add `matchExpressions` next to `matchLabels`; the API server labels every namespace with `kubernetes.io/metadata.name`. See `kubectl explain mutatingwebhookconfiguration.webhooks.namespaceSelector`. Keep the existing `matchLabels`: they are what keeps the webhook away from system namespaces.
