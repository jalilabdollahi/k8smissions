Three small additions to the CRD, nothing in the objects: `spec.names.categories`, `spec.names.shortNames`, and per-version `spec.versions[].additionalPrinterColumns` (each column needs `name`, `type` and a `jsonPath` into the object, e.g. into `.status`). See `kubectl explain crd.spec.names` and `kubectl explain crd.spec.versions.additionalPrinterColumns`. kubectl caches API discovery, so if your own terminal does not show the change, refresh it with `kubectl api-resources` or `--cache-dir`.
