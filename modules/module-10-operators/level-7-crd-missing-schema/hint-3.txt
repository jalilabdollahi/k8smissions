Declare every field the live Workloads use, not only the ones you want to constrain (or preserve the unknown ones explicitly on `spec`):

kubectl apply -f - <<'Y'
<the whole CRD, with spec.properties for replicas (integer, minimum), tier (string, enum), and the extra fields>
Y

`kubectl get crd workloads.platform.acme.io -o yaml` gives you the object to start from; remove the status and metadata noise before applying.
