A CRD without `properties` accepts anything because of `x-kubernetes-preserve-unknown-fields`. A schema (`openAPIV3Schema.properties`) lets you constrain fields with `type`, `enum`, `minimum` and friends. Be careful: in a structural schema every field you do not declare is pruned as unknown, in new writes and when stored objects are read back. Use `kubectl get workload <name> -o yaml` before and after a change, and `--dry-run=server` to test objects.
