Permissions come from Roles bound to the ServiceAccount: `kubectl -n k8smissions get role,rolebinding -o yaml`. Each rule names an API group, resources and verbs. The operator does three different things to three different resources, and one of them is a subresource: a rule for `reports` does not cover `reports/status`. Use `kubectl explain role.rules` for the fields. Fix one 403, re-read the log, and expect the next one; then compare with what the operator really needs and stop there.
