A pod runs as whatever `spec.serviceAccountName` says (`kubectl explain pod.spec.serviceAccountName`), and the default is `default`. Check with `kubectl auth can-i get configmap/flags --as=system:serviceaccount:k8smissions:flags-reader` that the prepared account is useful. If the next failure is a different message, look at `kubectl get sa flags-reader -o yaml` and at `kubectl explain pod.spec.automountServiceAccountToken`.
