Log in from a debug pod and repeat the request: `kubectl -n k8smissions exec probe -- sh -c 'curl -s -c /tmp/j "http://portal/login?user=a"; for i in 1 2 3 4 5 6; do curl -s -b /tmp/j http://portal/; done'`. Then ask which pod answered each time; `kubectl get svc portal -o yaml` shows how the Service balances.
