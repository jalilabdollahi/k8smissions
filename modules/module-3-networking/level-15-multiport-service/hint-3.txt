Two patches, one per object, and neither port may be dropped: `kubectl -n k8smissions patch ingress gateway --type=json -p '[{"op":"replace","path":"/spec/rules/0/http/paths/0/backend/service/port","value":{"name":"<service port name>"}}]'` and `kubectl -n k8smissions patch svc gateway --type=json -p '[{"op":"replace","path":"/spec/ports/<index>/protocol","value":"<protocol>"}]'`.
