The number of voluntary disruptions a budget allows is (healthy pods) minus (what it wants to keep). `minAvailable: 3` on 3 replicas allows zero evictions, so no node can ever be drained; the same is true for 1 and 1. A budget that allows exactly one disruption protects the service and lets drains through. The two workloads need different treatment because one has three pods and the other has one.
See: kubectl explain pdb.spec
