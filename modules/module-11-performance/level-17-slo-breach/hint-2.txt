Two probes do two different jobs. Readiness decides whether a pod receives Service traffic (it is removed from the endpoints when it fails, and added back when it passes); liveness decides whether the container is restarted. Only one of them is configured here, and it reacts after periodSeconds x failureThreshold. Work out how long that is, and how long a quarter of the traffic goes to a hung pod during that time. Careful with the other direction: a probe that fires on a single slow answer restarts healthy pods.
See: kubectl explain pod.spec.containers.readinessProbe  and  kubectl explain pod.spec.containers.livenessProbe
