Follow one request through the layers and measure each hop instead of guessing. Start with what the standing client sees, then look at each layer's own evidence:
  kubectl logs page-client --tail=3
  kubectl logs deploy/catalog-dns --tail=8                       (what does one page view cost the resolver?)
  kubectl exec deploy/pricing -- cat /sys/fs/cgroup/cpu.stat      (is pricing frozen by its CPU quota?)
  kubectl exec <each pricing pod> -- python -c "import urllib.request;print(urllib.request.urlopen('http://127.0.0.1:8080/stats').read())"
