There are three separate faults, one per layer, and fixing one only improves the number. DNS: look at the search domains and ndots in the storefront pod's resolv.conf and at how many queries one lookup of pricing.catalog.internal costs. CPU: each page costs pricing about 20ms of CPU, which a 100m limit cannot sustain at this rate (see cpu.stat). Routing: compare the request counters of the three pricing pods and read the Service spec for how connections from one client are assigned to pods.
See: kubectl explain service.spec.sessionAffinity  and  kubectl explain pod.spec.dnsConfig.options
