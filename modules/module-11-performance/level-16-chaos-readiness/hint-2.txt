A pod anti-affinity term says "do not schedule onto a node (topologyKey) that already runs a pod matching this labelSelector". If the selector matches no pod, the term is valid, accepted without any warning, and does nothing. Compare every key and value in the selector with the labels the pods really carry.
See: kubectl explain pod.spec.affinity.podAntiAffinity.requiredDuringSchedulingIgnoredDuringExecution
