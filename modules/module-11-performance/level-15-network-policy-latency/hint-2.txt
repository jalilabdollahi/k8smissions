A DNS answer that does not fit in a 512 byte UDP reply comes back truncated (the TC flag) and the resolver is expected to repeat the question over TCP. Read the NetworkPolicy's egress ports for the protocol it lists, and think about what the client does after the truncated reply and how long it waits for a TCP connection that never completes.
See: kubectl describe networkpolicy price-feed-egress  and  kubectl explain networkpolicy.spec.egress.ports
