Total time is roughly (number of waves) x (time for one wave). The number of waves depends on how many pods may be replaced at once (maxSurge extra pods, maxUnavailable fewer pods). The length of a wave is the container start, the readiness probe's initialDelaySeconds and the Deployment's minReadySeconds. Both parts matter, and the capacity floor (30 of 40 available) limits how aggressive maxUnavailable can be.
See: kubectl explain deployment.spec.strategy.rollingUpdate  and  kubectl explain deployment.spec.minReadySeconds
