Kubernetes sends SIGTERM to PID 1 of each container and waits terminationGracePeriodSeconds before SIGKILL. Two things decide whether a 20 second clip survives: whether the signal reaches the program that handles it (a shell as PID 1 does not forward signals), and whether the grace period is longer than the longest piece of work the worker may be doing.
See: kubectl explain pod.spec.terminationGracePeriodSeconds and kubectl explain pod.spec.containers.command
