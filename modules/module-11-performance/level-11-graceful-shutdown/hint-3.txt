Make the worker PID 1 and give it time to finish its longest clip:
  kubectl patch deployment video-transcoder --type=json -p '[
    {"op":"replace","path":"/spec/template/spec/containers/0/command","value":[<run the worker directly, no shell>]},
    {"op":"replace","path":"/spec/template/spec/terminationGracePeriodSeconds","value":<longer than the longest clip>}]'
(`exec <program>` inside a shell wrapper is the other way to get the same PID 1.)
