Delete a transcoder pod on purpose and watch how it dies, then compare with what the worker itself logs on SIGTERM:
  kubectl delete pod <one video-transcoder pod> & kubectl logs -f <the same pod>
  kubectl get --raw /api/v1/namespaces/k8smissions/services/result-collector:8081/proxy/ | head -c 300
