Both pods are killed for memory, but the memory curves differ. Watch each one over time rather than once, and read how each container last died:
  kubectl top pod --containers          (repeat it a few times, or watch -n 5)
  kubectl describe pod -l app=profile-api | grep -A6 "Last State"
  kubectl logs deploy/profile-api --previous
Does the number rise steadily and forever, or jump to a value and stay there?
