Check what each pod is told to ask. Gateway pods use dnsPolicy None, so their resolv.conf comes straight from dnsConfig: compare its nameserver with the ClusterIP of each Service (kubectl get svc). Then read the cache's Corefile, in particular the options of the cache plugin: how long may an answer be kept? A running CoreDNS only reads its Corefile at start.
See: kubectl explain pod.spec.dnsConfig
