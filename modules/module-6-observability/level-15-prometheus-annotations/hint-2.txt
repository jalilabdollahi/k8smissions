prom-lite lists pods, so only annotations on the Pod object count; annotations on the Deployment's own metadata never reach its pods. Annotation values are strings (`kubectl explain pod.metadata.annotations`), and the scraper reads `prometheus.io/scrape`, `prometheus.io/port` and `prometheus.io/path`. Once the target appears, its `lastError` tells you whether the port and path are right; the app's own log says where it serves metrics.
