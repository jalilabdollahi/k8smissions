Put the three annotations in the pod template: `kubectl -n k8smissions patch deploy payments-api --type=merge -p '{"spec":{"template":{"metadata":{"annotations":{"prometheus.io/scrape":"<string>","prometheus.io/port":"<port>","prometheus.io/path":"<path>"}}}}}'`. Quote every value, including the boolean one, then check `/targets` again once the new pods are up.
