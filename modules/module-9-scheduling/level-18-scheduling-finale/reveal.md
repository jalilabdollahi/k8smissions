1. `shop-frontend`: its spread constraint uses `topologyKey: zone` with `minDomains: 2`, and only one worker has a `zone` label, so only one domain exists and only one pod fits. Label the other worker: `kubectl label node <other-worker> zone=zone-b` (changing the key to hostname and dropping `minDomains` also works).
2. `shop-cart`: its required anti-affinity selects `app=shop`, which every frontend pod also carries. With frontends on both workers, no node is free for the second cart pod. Narrow it to `{app: shop, tier: cart}`:
   `kubectl -n k8smissions patch deploy shop-cart --type=json -p '[{"op":"replace","path":"/spec/template/spec/affinity/podAntiAffinity/requiredDuringSchedulingIgnoredDuringExecution/0/labelSelector/matchLabels","value":{"app":"shop","tier":"cart"}}]'`
3. `shop-agent`: `statsd-debug`, a leftover capture pod, holds hostPort 8125 on one worker. Delete it: `kubectl -n k8smissions delete pod statsd-debug`.
