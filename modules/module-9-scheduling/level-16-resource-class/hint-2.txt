Three objects have to agree: a DeviceClass (selects devices by CEL, e.g. on `device.driver`), the ResourceClaimTemplate (names the class and may add its own CEL selectors), and the ResourceSlices (what each node offers: driver, pool, device name, attributes). Compare the driver and attribute domain in the slices with every selector. `kubectl explain deviceclass.spec`. A template's spec cannot be edited in place, and a pod's claim is generated when the pod is created.
