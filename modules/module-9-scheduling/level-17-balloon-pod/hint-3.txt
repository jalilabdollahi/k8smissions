Give the placeholders a PriorityClass below the default of 0 (a `PriorityClass` with a negative `value`), set it as `priorityClassName` in the `capacity-reserve` pod template with `kubectl -n k8smissions patch deploy capacity-reserve --type=merge -p '{"spec":{"template":{"spec":{"priorityClassName":"<name>"}}}}'`, and let the scheduler preempt. Deleting the placeholders is not accepted.
