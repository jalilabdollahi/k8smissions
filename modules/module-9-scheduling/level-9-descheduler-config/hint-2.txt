In the v1alpha2 policy, every plugin must be enabled under the extension point it implements (`deschedule` for per-pod rules, `balance` for cluster-level rebalancing). Once the policy loads, LowNodeUtilization only moves pods if some node counts as underutilized: it must sit below ALL of the `thresholds` percentages. Compare them with what the idle worker really has (`kubectl describe node`, Allocated resources).
