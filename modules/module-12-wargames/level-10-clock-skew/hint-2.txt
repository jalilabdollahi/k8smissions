A node clock cannot differ between pods on the same node, but a process can still format "now" differently: a time zone in the environment, with a timestamp string that carries no zone, is read by the other side as UTC. Separately, a token that lives 5 seconds is useless to a client that takes longer than that, and `api` has a leeway setting for small differences. Once expiry is sorted, read the error again: the next one is about the signature, and two Secrets, `auth-signing` and `api-verify`, carry `rotation/kid` annotations (`kubectl explain pod.spec.containers.env.valueFrom.secretKeyRef`).
