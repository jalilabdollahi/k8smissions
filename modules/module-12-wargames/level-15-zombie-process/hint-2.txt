Processes in state `Z` are zombies: finished, waiting for their parent to collect the exit status. Orphans are adopted by PID 1 of the container, and here PID 1 is a Python HTTP server that never calls `wait()`; the per-user process cap fills up and the pod can no longer start threads or processes. `kubectl explain pod.spec.shareProcessNamespace` describes a way to give the pod a PID 1 that reaps. Separately, nothing tells Kubernetes the pod is wedged: look at `kubectl explain pod.spec.containers.readinessProbe` and `livenessProbe`, and think about which of them removes a pod from the Service and which one restarts it.
