Read the password from a running pod: `kubectl -n k8smissions exec <running billing-api pod> -- printenv DB_PASSWORD`. Then recreate the Secret with every key the consumers reference: `kubectl -n k8smissions create secret generic db-credentials --from-literal=<key1>=<password> --from-literal=<key2>=<password>`. Check the keys with the `secretKeyRef` lines of the Deployment and the CronJob.
