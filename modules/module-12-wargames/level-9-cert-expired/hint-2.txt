Both certificates are expired, and the failure moves from one side to the other as you fix them (mutual TLS checks both directions). New certificates must be signed by the internal CA, whose key is in Secret `internal-ca`: `openssl x509 -req -CA ca.crt -CAkey ca.key ...` with an `-extfile` carrying the server's names (`subjectAltName`; copy them from the old certificate with `openssl x509 -text`). Then think about how each Deployment mounts its Secret: `kubectl get deploy ledger -o yaml` (`kubectl explain pod.spec.containers.volumeMounts.subPath`).
