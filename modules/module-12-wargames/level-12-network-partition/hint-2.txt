The first error is a name lookup failure, not a refused connection: ask what a pod needs to be allowed to do to resolve `kv-1.kv-peers...` when egress is restricted. After that, compare the ports in the NetworkPolicy with the ports the container declares (`kubectl get sts kv -o yaml`). When names still do not resolve even though traffic is allowed, remember that a headless Service lists only Ready pods by default, and these pods become Ready only after they find each other: `kubectl explain service.spec.publishNotReadyAddresses`.
