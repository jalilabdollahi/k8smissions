Three separate things sit between the app and its data: where the pod may run (a volume remembers its node, see `kubectl get pv -o yaml` for `nodeAffinity` and `kubectl get nodes`), whether the Service `ledger-db` has endpoints (`kubectl get endpointslices`, compare its selector with `kubectl get pod ledger-db-0 --show-labels`), and what the NetworkPolicy lets in (`kubectl explain networkpolicy.spec.ingress.from`). Do not delete the PVC: the booked entries live on it.
