The namespace enforces `restricted`: every container needs `allowPrivilegeEscalation: false`, `capabilities.drop: [ALL]`, and the pod needs `runAsNonRoot: true` with a real `runAsUser` (the image runs as root) and a seccomp profile (`kubectl explain pod.spec.securityContext`); do not weaken the namespace label. Scheduling: compare the `nodeSelector` with `kubectl get nodes --show-labels`, then the taints (`kubectl describe node | grep -A2 Taints`). Probes: the app takes about 25 seconds to listen, and a liveness probe that fails twice at 5 second intervals kills it first; `kubectl explain pod.spec.containers.startupProbe`. Autoscaling: which metric does the HPA use, how many replicas may it create, and does every container have the request that a utilisation percentage is computed against? `kubectl explain hpa.spec.metrics`.
