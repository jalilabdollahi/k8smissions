Work through the symptoms in order and re-check after each fix, because each hides the next. No pods: `kubectl -n checkout describe rs -l app=checkout` (Events) and `kubectl get ns checkout --show-labels`. Pending: `kubectl -n checkout describe pod`. Running but never Ready: `kubectl -n checkout describe pod` (probe events, restart count) and `kubectl -n checkout logs <pod> -c app`. No scaling: `kubectl -n checkout describe hpa checkout` and `kubectl top pod -n checkout --containers`.
