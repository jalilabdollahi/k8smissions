Four independent problems, in dependency order: the ServiceAccount named by the pod template; the PVC's StorageClass (`kubectl get storageclass`; `kubectl explain pvc.spec.storageClassName`, a PVC cannot change it, and a PVC that a pod still references stays `Terminating` until that pod is gone); the migration Job, whose pod template is immutable and whose argument is checked against the schema the web pods wait for (read the Job's pod log and the init container log of a web pod); and the Ingress backend, which must name a port the Service really has (`kubectl get svc shop-web -o yaml`).
