No pods at all means the Deployment's ReplicaSet could not create them: `kubectl -n k8smissions describe rs -l app=shop-web` (Events). Then look at everything the pod template depends on: `kubectl -n k8smissions get sa,pvc,job,svc,ingress` and `kubectl -n k8smissions describe pvc shop-data`. The ingress controller's own log is `kubectl -n ingress-nginx logs deploy/ingress-nginx-controller`.
