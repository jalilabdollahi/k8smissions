`kubectl -n k8smissions create serviceaccount <name from the pod template>`. For storage: delete Job `db-migrate` first (its pod holds the claim), then `kubectl delete pvc shop-data`, then re-create it with a StorageClass from `kubectl get storageclass`. Re-create the Job from `kubectl get job db-migrate -o yaml` with the schema argument the web pods wait for (`kubectl logs <pod> -c wait-migration`). Finally `kubectl -n k8smissions patch ingress shop --type=json -p='[{"op":"replace","path":"/spec/rules/0/http/paths/0/backend/service/port","value":{"name":"<port name of the Service>"}}]'`.
