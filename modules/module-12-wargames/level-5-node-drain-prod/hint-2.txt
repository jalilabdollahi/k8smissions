Each message maps to one object: a PodDisruptionBudget that can never be satisfied (`kubectl get pdb` and compare ALLOWED DISRUPTIONS with replicas), a pod with no owner and a pod with an `emptyDir` (see the `drain` flags in `kubectl drain --help`), and, before you celebrate, check where the evicted pods are supposed to go: `kubectl describe node` on the other worker (Taints) and `kubectl get pods` after the drain.
