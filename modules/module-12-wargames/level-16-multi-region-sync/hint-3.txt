Free the lease so a live pod can take it: `kubectl -n eu delete lease eu-leader` (or patch `spec.holderIdentity` and `spec.leaseDurationSeconds`). Give the `eu` namespace the label the policy asks for: `kubectl label namespace eu region=<value from the policy>`. Recreate the `us` Secret without a trailing newline (`kubectl -n us create secret generic replication-token --from-literal=token=<value> --dry-run=client -o yaml | kubectl apply -f -`, or `printf`, never `echo`), then `kubectl -n us rollout restart deploy/store`. Do not delete the NetworkPolicy.
