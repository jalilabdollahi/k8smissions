A Lease is held until `renewTime` plus `leaseDurationSeconds`; read both next to the holder name, and `kubectl get pods -n eu` for the holder. Next, the `us` NetworkPolicy admits namespaces by label: compare `kubectl get ns --show-labels` with the policy's `namespaceSelector`. Finally, a replica that answers 401 does not like the token: compare the two Secrets byte for byte, for example `kubectl -n us get secret replication-token -o jsonpath='{.data.token}' | base64 -d | od -c`, and remember what `echo` appends and when a pod reads a Secret exposed as an environment variable.
