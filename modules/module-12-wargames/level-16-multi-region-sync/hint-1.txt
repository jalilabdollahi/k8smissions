Follow the data: `kubectl -n eu logs deploy/replicator` says whether a replicator is replicating at all and why not (each pod prints its own state, so check both pods). `kubectl -n eu get lease eu-leader -o yaml` shows who holds the lease and for how long. Later on the same logs report what happens when it tries to reach `us`; `kubectl -n us logs deploy/store` reports what the replica thinks of the requests it does get.
