Freeing Job slots is only a temporary cure while the retrier keeps filling them: `kubectl explain job.spec.ttlSecondsAfterFinished` is how finished Jobs remove themselves, and the retrier takes its value from an environment variable. Once Job slots are free, `release-v42` still has a pod to run, and the other quota (`requests.cpu`) decides whether that pod may exist: check which running pod is holding most of it. Pods of a Job are deleted with the Job, so deleting pods by hand frees nothing.
