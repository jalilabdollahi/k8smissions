Create a new ConfigMap with the last good values (`kubectl -n k8smissions create cm shop-config-v43 --from-literal=MAX_CONN=<value from v41> --from-literal=PRICING_URL=<value from v41>`), then point every consumer at it: `kubectl -n k8smissions patch deploy <name> --type=json -p='[{"op":"replace","path":"<path to the configMap name>","value":"shop-config-v43"}]'` for each Deployment, and `kubectl patch cronjob` for the CronJob (the volume is under `spec.template.spec.volumes`, `envFrom` under the container).
