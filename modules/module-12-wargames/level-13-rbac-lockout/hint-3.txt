`kubectl -n k8smissions set serviceaccount deploy/ledger-sync <new account>`; `kubectl -n k8smissions patch sa <new account> -p '{"automountServiceAccountToken":true}'`; `kubectl -n k8smissions patch rolebinding <name> --type=json -p='[{"op":"replace","path":"/subjects/0/name","value":"<new account>"}]'`; and `kubectl -n platform-config create rolebinding <name> --role=<existing role there> --serviceaccount=k8smissions:<new account>`. Pods created before an account change keep the old setup: restart them with `kubectl -n k8smissions rollout restart deploy/ledger-sync`. Do not bind a ClusterRole such as cluster-admin: the account must stay least-privilege.
