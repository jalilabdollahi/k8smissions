There are three faults below the gateway: `auth` reads its signing key from a file that has to exist in the mounted Secret (`kubectl get secret auth-secrets -o yaml`, keys are the file names), `users-cache` is waiting on a volume (`kubectl describe pod` Events and `kubectl get cm`), and once it runs the Service in front of it must reach the port the container really listens on (`kubectl get svc,endpointslices`, `kubectl explain service.spec.ports.targetPort`).
