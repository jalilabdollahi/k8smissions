Give the Secret the key the app expects: `kubectl -n k8smissions patch secret auth-secrets -p '{"data":{"<file name>":"<base64 value copied from the existing key>"}}'`. Point the volume at the ConfigMap that exists with `kubectl -n k8smissions patch deploy users-cache --type=json -p='[{"op":"replace","path":"/spec/template/spec/volumes/1/configMap/name","value":"<name>"}]'`, and fix `targetPort` with `kubectl -n k8smissions patch svc users-cache --type=json -p='[{"op":"replace","path":"/spec/ports/0/targetPort","value":<port>}]'`.
