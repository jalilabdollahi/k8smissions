Make the two paths agree. Either move the mount:

  kubectl patch deployment web-app -n k8smissions --type=json \
    -p '[{"op":"replace","path":"/spec/template/spec/containers/0/volumeMounts/1/mountPath","value":"<path-the-app-writes-to>"}]'

or point the app at the mount with kubectl set env deployment/web-app DATA_DIR=<mountPath>. Either way the pod rolls out again.
