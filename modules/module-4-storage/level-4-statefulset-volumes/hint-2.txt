A StatefulSet normally gets one PVC per pod from volumeClaimTemplates (named <template>-<statefulset>-<ordinal>). This one lists a single, fixed claim under volumes, so every pod mounts the same directory.

  kubectl get statefulset postgres-cluster -n k8smissions -o yaml
  kubectl explain statefulset.spec.volumeClaimTemplates

Remember which StatefulSet fields can be changed after creation.
