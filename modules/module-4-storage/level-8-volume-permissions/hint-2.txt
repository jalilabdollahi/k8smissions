The key file is root-owned and its mode is 0400 (owner read only), so uid 10001 is neither the owner nor, by default, in the file's group. Kubernetes can give the volume's files to a group of your choosing for the pod.

  kubectl explain pod.spec.securityContext.fsGroup
  kubectl explain pod.spec.volumes.secret.defaultMode

Be careful with widening the mode for everyone: this app refuses a key that others can read.
