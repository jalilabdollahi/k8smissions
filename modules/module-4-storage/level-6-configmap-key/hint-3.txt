Make the key the volume selects match a key that exists in app-config (or make the ConfigMap hold that key), while the file still shows up as app.yaml:

  kubectl patch deploy/web-app --namespace k8smissions --type=json \
    -p '[{"op":"replace","path":"/spec/template/spec/volumes/1/configMap/items/0/key","value":"<existing-key>"}]'
