Either point the Deployment at a claim that exists, or create the claim it asks for:

  kubectl patch deployment orders-db -n k8smissions --type=json \
    -p '[{"op":"replace","path":"/spec/template/spec/volumes/0/persistentVolumeClaim/claimName","value":"<existing-claim>"}]'

or apply a PersistentVolumeClaim with that exact name (ReadWriteOnce, 1Gi) in the k8smissions namespace.
