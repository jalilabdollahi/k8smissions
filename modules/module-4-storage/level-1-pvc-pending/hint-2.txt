Compare two names: the claim the pod's volume asks for (spec.volumes[].persistentVolumeClaim.claimName) and the claims that actually exist (kubectl get pvc).

A claim on the default StorageClass shows Pending until a pod uses it (volumeBindingMode: WaitForFirstConsumer), so Pending on its own is not a fault. Check with: kubectl get sc standard -o jsonpath='{.volumeBindingMode}'
